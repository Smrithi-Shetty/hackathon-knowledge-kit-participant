# Borrowing from a HELOC Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Homeownership](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/homeownership)

![Borrowing from a HELOC Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Homeownership-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Tapping into your home's equity is a smart way to fund renovations, consolidate debt, or cover major expenses. Use this [Borrowing from a HELOC Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/borrowing-from-a-heloc)

calculator to estimate your ongoing payments for a Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC). By adjusting the borrowed amount and interest rate, you can prepare your budget for the repayment phase. See how easily you can leverage your home's value to achieve your other financial goals.
