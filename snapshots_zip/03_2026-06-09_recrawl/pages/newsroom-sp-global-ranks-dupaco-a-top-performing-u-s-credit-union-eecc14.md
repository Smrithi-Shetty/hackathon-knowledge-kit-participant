# S&P Global ranks Dupaco a top-performing U.S. credit union

[In the News (Mentions)](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/in-the-news-mentions)

![S&P Global ranks Dupaco a top-performing U.S. credit union](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/placeholder-no-image.png)

DUBUQUE, Iowa—S&P Global Market Intelligence has ranked Dupaco Community Credit Union a top-performing credit union in the United States.

S&P Global Market Intelligence analyzed 1,727 credit unions and recognized 100 credit unions, including just four in Iowa. The credit union’s performance was calculated based on five metrics:

1. Member growth;
2. Average loans, net of Paycheck Protection Program loans, per member;
3. Net worth as a percentage of total assets;
4. Delinquent loans as a percentage of total loans, net of PPP loans; and
5. Return on average asset

“This recognition simply reaffirms our staff consistently delivers on the Dupaco brand promise to improve each member’s financial position and uplift the communities we serve,” said Dupaco President and CEO Joe Hearn. “When we do that, the financial numbers take care of themselves.”

Credit unions under $100 million in total assets were excluded from the analysis.

Click [here](https://platform.mi.spglobal.com/web/client?auth=inherit#news/article?id=63079494&cdid=A-63079494-10030) for the S&P Global rankings and scores, published March 23, 2021.

**ABOUT DUPACO COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION:**

Dupaco Community Credit Union is a not-for-profit, member-owned financial cooperative. It is dedicated to the financial sustainability of its members, and specializes in customized financial counseling, money advice and education. It offers savings, loans, investments, insurance and wealth management products for individuals and businesses. Dupaco serves residents in 112 counties throughout Iowa, northwest Illinois and southwest Wisconsin. Chartered in 1948, membership totals more than 137,000 with assets exceeding $2.5 billion. Visit Dupaco at [www.dupaco.com](https://www.dupaco.com/).
