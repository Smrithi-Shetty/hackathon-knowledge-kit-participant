# Loan Term Comparison Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Homeownership](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/homeownership)

![Loan Term Comparison Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Homeownership-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Choosing a 15-year or 30-year mortgage will drastically alter your financial future. Use our  [Loan Term Comparison Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/loan-term-comparison) to see exactly how different loan terms affect your monthly payment and your total interest paid over time. While a shorter term means higher monthly payments, it can save you tens of thousands of dollars in interest. Compare your options side-by-side to find the perfect balance between monthly cash flow and long-term savings.
