# Dupaco welcomes Brian Henneger, VP Mortgage Lending

[Awards & Recognition](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/awards-recognition) [Career & Income](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/career-income) [Dupaco Updates](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/dupaco-updates)

MADISON, Wis.—Dupaco Community Credit Union is pleased to welcome Brian Henneger to the financial cooperative as VP, Mortgage Lending. Based at Dupaco’s Mineral Point branch in Madison, Henneger will serve members across the Madison area, helping individuals and families navigate the homebuying process.

Henneger brings 24 years of experience in the mortgage industry and deep expertise across a broad range of lending needs—including purchases, refinances, construction lending, investment properties, and VA and FHA loans. Through Dupaco, he’ll be helping support members’ homeownership goals at every stage of life.

“We’re excited to welcome Brian to our team,” said Jill Schweikert, Dupaco SVP, Consumer & Mortgage Lending. “His extensive experience, thoughtful approach and commitment to member education make him a strong fit for Dupaco and our members in the Madison area.”

###

ABOUT DUPACO COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION

Dupaco Community Credit Union is a not-for-profit, member-owned financial cooperative headquartered in Dubuque, Iowa. It is dedicated to the financial well-being of its members, and specializes in personalized financial counseling, money advice and education. It offers savings, loans, investments, insurance and wealth management products for individuals and businesses. Dupaco serves residents in 118 counties throughout Iowa, northwest Illinois and southwest Wisconsin. Founded in 1948 by ten Dubuque Packing Company employees, membership has grown to more than 181,000 with assets exceeding $3.6 billion. It has over 600 employees and 22 branch office locations. Equal Housing Opportunity. Learn more at [www.dupaco.com](https://www.dupaco.com/).
