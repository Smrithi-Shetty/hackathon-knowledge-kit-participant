# Burn Rate Calculator

[Tools](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/tools) [Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/calculators)

![Burn Rate Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/calculator-hero-graphic-icon-web-template.jpg)

Managing your cash flow is critical to keeping your doors open and your business thriving. Download this easy-to-use [Burn Rate Template](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Burn-Rate-Dupaco%20(1).xlsx) to calculate the exact date when your business will likely run out of cash, also known as your cash-zero date. By understanding this timeline early, you can make proactive decisions to cut costs, secure additional financing, and extend your financial runway.
