## Who’s Who on Your Account

If you’re teaming up on an account, it’s helpful to know who does what! Here’s a quick breakdown:

- **Primary member/account owner:** Every membership starts with a primary member. You open your share with a one-time $25 deposit linked to your SSN or Tax ID. This membership is exclusively yours, giving you full responsibility and control over your accounts while you’re living.
- **Joint/secondary account owner:** You’re the support team—like a partner, parent or co-borrower added to an account by the primary member. Your access is defined in your signed agreement. You can open your own membership share, but it won’t change access to accounts you’ve been added to.

### **Primary vs. Joint: What's the diff?**

## **Here's an overview of how privileges work**

| Privileges | Primary Member | Joint/Secondary Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access and manage the shared account through Shine, over the phone, and in-branch | Yes | Yes (with their own login or identity authentication credentials) |
| Perform day-to-day banking transactions on the shared account (like deposits, withdrawals, transfers, etc.) | Yes | Yes |
| Remove joint/secondary owners from the shared account | Yes | Yes (with the Primary's verbal authorization) |
| Open and add new accounts, loans, and lines of credit to the primary member's membership | Yes | No |
| Access and manage all accounts associated with the primary member's SSN or TIN | Yes | No (just the ones they're added to) |
| Close a shared account, loan, or line of credit | Yes | Yes (unless it's a Share Savings account) |
| Close a primary member's membership | Yes | No |
