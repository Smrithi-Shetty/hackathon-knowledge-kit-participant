# Mini-Max Financing Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Auto](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/auto)

![Mini-Max Financing Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Auto-motorcycle-loan-HGicon-676x338.webp)

Want the lower payments of a lease but the flexibility of vehicle ownership? Use this tool to calculate a monthly payment with Dupaco’s unique, lease-like Mini-Max financing option. This alternative allows you to pay only for the vehicle's depreciation over your loan term, resulting in significantly lower monthly costs. Explore this flexible option to get more car for your money without the rigid restrictions of a traditional lease.
