COMMON FEATURES
Bylaw Requirements. You must complete payment of one share in a Savings (Share) account as a condition of admission
to membership.
Par Value of Shares. The par value of a share in the credit union is $25.00.
Nature of Dividends. Dividends are paid from current income and available earnings, after required transfers to reserves
at the end of a dividend period. Not applicable to term share or share certificate accounts.
National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund. Member accounts in this credit union are federally insured by the National
Credit Union Share Insurance Fund.
