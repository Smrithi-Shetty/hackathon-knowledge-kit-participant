# How live video tellers work and what they can do for you

[Budgeting](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/budgeting) [Investing & Retirement](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/investing-retirement) [Borrowing & Credit](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/borrowing-credit)

![How live video tellers work and what they can do for you](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Photos/Branches/Dupaco-Waukee-Branch-Interior-1-202504.jpeg)

You may not always have time to visit a credit union branch during open hours. Maybe you’re running errands before work or need to make a quick deposit afterwards. That’s one way that live video tellers can come in handy.

These machines connect you to a real Dupaco Credit Union teller—face-to-face on screen—so you can handle most of your everyday banking needs quickly and conveniently. If you’ve ever wondered how live video tellers work (or what you can do with them), here’s what to know.

## **How does a live video teller work?**

A live video teller, sometimes called an Interactive Teller Machine (ITM), looks a lot like an ATM. But it connects you to a real Dupaco teller through a video screen.

Just tap the screen to start. A teller will then appear on the screen to help you in real time. You’ll see and hear each other just like a video call, while the teller securely controls the machine to process your transaction.

Some of Dupaco’s ITMs are located outside in drive-up lanes, perfect for quick stops on the go. Others are found inside branch lobbies, offering the same personal service—just through the screen.

It’s the same friendly experience you’d get at the counter, simply in another way.

## **What you can do with a live video teller**

You can take care of almost all the same transactions you’d handle with an in-person teller, including:

- Deposit cash or checks
- Withdraw cash
- Make loan payments
- Transfer money between accounts
- Order checks

“Our members can do just about all the same transactions they would if a teller at the branch were helping them,” said Dupaco’s Collin Olson.

## **Do I need my debit card to use one?**

Nope. Just tap the screen to connect with a live video teller. They’ll verify your identity.

## **Is it difficult to use?**

Most members are surprised by how natural it feels.

> “Many of our members are amazed by how a real person actually pops up on the screen to assist them, how easy they are to use and how much the tellers can do for them,” Olson said.

If you’ve ever used video chat, you’ll feel right at home.

## **Why members use live video tellers**

Live video tellers aren’t meant to replace people—they’re here to make your life easier and your experience more flexible.

They offer benefits like:

- **Extended hours:** Speak with a live teller before or after traditional lobby hours.
- **Personal help:** You’re still talking with a real teller, not a chatbot or automated system.
- **Convenient access:** Available both in drive-up lanes and inside some branches.
- **Faster service:** Handle your transaction quickly, sometimes without leaving your car.

And because routine transactions can be handled through live video tellers, Dupaco’s in-branch teams have more time for deeper one-on-one conversations, like helping you:

- Build or improve your credit with a free [Credit History Lesson](https://www.dupaco.com/about/member-services)
- Review your finances with a free [Money Makeover](https://www.dupaco.com/about/member-services)
- Create a plan to help you reach your next financial goal

## **Where to find a live video teller**

You’ll find live video tellers at many Dupaco locations—some outside in drive-up lanes, others right inside the branch.

Either way, you’ll always connect with a real person ready to help.

[Find your closest live video teller >](https://www.dupaco.com/contact#dupaco-locator-widget_1769206785258)

## **Still prefer coming inside? That’s OK.**

Live video tellers are simply one more way to stay connected to your credit union—along with [Shine Online and Mobile banking](https://www.dupaco.com/shine), in-branch visits and more.

No matter how you choose to bank, you have access to real people who care about helping you reach your goals.
