# Lease vs. Buy Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Auto](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/auto)

![Lease vs. Buy Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Auto-motorcycle-loan-HGicon-676x338.webp)

Deciding whether to lease or finance your next vehicle depends heavily on your driving habits and financial goals. Use our [Lease vs. Buy Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/lease-vs-buy) to easily compare the expenses of buying vs. leasing your next ride over the exact same time period. By breaking down the monthly payments, depreciation, and upfront costs, you can see which option saves you the most money. Make an informed decision that aligns with both your lifestyle and your wallet.
