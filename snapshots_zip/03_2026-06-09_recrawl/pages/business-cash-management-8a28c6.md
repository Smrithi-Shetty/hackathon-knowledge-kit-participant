## Frequently asked questions about business cash management

- [What is business cash management?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

Business cash management includes the tools and services that help you accept payments, make payments and move money efficiently. It’s all about keeping your cash flowing and your business running smoothly.

- [How can business cash management help improve my cash flow?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

With the right business cash management solutions, you can get paid faster, automate payments and reduce delays. That means better visibility into your money and fewer cash-flow surprises.

- [What payment options can I use to accept payments from customers?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

Dupaco offers several ways to accept payments, including merchant services for in-store, online and mobile payments, ACH payment solutions and netWork Deposit for remote check deposits.

- [What tools can help me pay bills and vendors more efficiently?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

You can use Bill Pay to pay invoices online and automate recurring payments, ACH payment solutions for electronic withdrawals and wire transfers to send money securely anywhere in the world.

- [Is business cash management secure?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

Yes. Our business cash management tools are designed with security in mind to help protect your transactions and sensitive information while moving money quickly and reliably.

- [Can business cash management solutions be tailored to my business?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

Absolutely. Whether your payment needs are simple or more complex, we’ll help you find business cash management solutions that fit how your business operates.

- [What information is required for wire transfers?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/cash-management#)

To ensure your funds move quickly and securely, please have the following details ready:

To send a wire:

  - **Recipient details:** Beneficiary’s full name, physical address and account number.

  - **Bank details:** Receiving bank’s name, address and ABA routing number.

  - **Transfer amount:** Dollar amount of wire transfer.

  - **International wires:** Physical address for the financial institution, sender and beneficiary.

  - **Security:** A Personal Identification Number (PIN) is required for all transfers initiated by phone.

To receive a wire:

  - **Our routing number:** Provide the payer with our ABA number (273974581).

  - **Account info:** Your business name, physical address and the account number where the funds should be deposited.
