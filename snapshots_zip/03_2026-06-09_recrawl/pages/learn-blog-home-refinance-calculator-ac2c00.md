# Home Refinance Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Homeownership](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/homeownership)

![Home Refinance Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Homeownership-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Refinancing your mortgage can free up monthly cash flow, but you need to make sure the closing costs are worth it. Use our [Home Refinance Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/home-refinance) to calculate whether it makes financial sense to refinance your current home loan based on today's interest rates. By factoring in your break-even point, you can see exactly how long it will take for the monthly savings to outweigh the initial fees. Crunch the numbers to see if a better mortgage is within reach.
