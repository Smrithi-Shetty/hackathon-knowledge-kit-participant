## Frequently asked questions about business auto loans and equipment loans

- [What can I use a business auto loan for?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

A business auto loan can be used to purchase work vehicles like trucks, vans or fleet vehicles your business relies on to operate and grow.

- [What types of equipment qualify for an equipment loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

Equipment loans can be used for many business needs, including construction equipment, machinery, seasonal equipment and other essential tools your business depends on.

- [How much do I need for a down payment?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

A minimum 20% down payment is required for business auto loans and business equipment loans.

- [What are the loan terms?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

Loan terms can be up to seven years, depending on your application and the type of vehicle or equipment you’re financing.

- [Are the interest rates fixed or variable?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

Our business auto loans and business equipment loans come with fixed interest rates for up to five years, so your payments stay predictable.

- [Can I make payments online?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/equipment-vehicle-loans#)

Yes. You can make your loan payments online anytime, making it easy to stay on top of your loan wherever you are.
