##### **Dream to Reality: Maker Mentorship Program**

# Have an Idea You'd Love to Create?

Dupaco and Key City Creative Center (KCCC) are partnering to help one aspiring maker turn a creative dream into reality. Through one-on-one mentorship, you'll work with KCCC experts to take your idea from concept to completed project. Whether you envision a woodworking piece, welded sculpture, 3D printed innovation or something else, this program could help bring your project to life.

![Amber](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Photos/Community/Amber-CNC.jpg?width=1418&height=944&name=Amber-CNC.jpg)

#### Application period: Oct. 14–Nov. 11, 2026

##### **About the Program**

## From Inspiration to Completion

Have a creative project you've always wanted to tackle but aren't sure where to start? The Dream to Reality: Maker Mentorship Program connects one local maker with experienced [KCCC](https://keycitycreativecenter.org/) mentors who will provide hands-on guidance every step of the way. Together, you'll develop a plan, learn new skills, overcome challenges and create something you're proud of—all while gaining valuable experience in a supportive makerspace environment.

![Orange graphic of a person.](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Icons/Simplified%20icons/user.svg)

#### Could It Be You?

One local maker will be selected for this opportunity. If chosen, you'll work toward a project you've been dreaming about with dedicated support from start to finish.

![Orange graphic of an outlined checkmark.](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Icons/Simplified%20icons/CheckFat.svg)

#### Get Expert Help

Receive personalized one-on-one mentorship from [KCCC](https://keycitycreativecenter.org/) experts who will help you build skills, solve challenges and confidently move your idea forward from beginning to end.

![star](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/star.svg)

#### Bring It to Life

Access KCCC's tools, equipment and project materials as you create. From January through June 2027, you'll create a finished project you can call your own.

![Orange graphic of a notepad.](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Icons/Simplified%20icons/Notepad.svg)

#### Share Your Story

As part of the program, you'll share your journey and completed project through photos, videos or interviews. Together, we'll celebrate your progress and showcase your experience.

![KCCC winner](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Photos/Community/KCCC-homepage-winner-announcement-e1654630911299-Apr-02-2026-04-03-01-2417-AM.jpg?width=720&height=360&name=KCCC-homepage-winner-announcement-e1654630911299-Apr-02-2026-04-03-01-2417-AM.jpg)

## Who Should Apply?

No matter your experience level, we're looking for applicants with a compelling idea, a willingness to learn and a commitment to seeing their project through to completion. This opportunity is ideal for:

- Makers with a creative project they've been wanting to build

- Individuals eager to learn new skills and techniques

- Artists, hobbyists and aspiring entrepreneurs

- Anyone looking for expert guidance to bring an idea to life


### Powered by Partnership

Behind every great project is a community that believes in it. Together, [KCCC](https://keycitycreativecenter.org/) and Dupaco are helping one local maker gain the tools, support and encouragement to turn an idea into reality.

![KCCC-logo](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Graphics/Logos/KCCC-logo.png?width=150&height=150&name=KCCC-logo.png)

#### Create at KCCC

[KCCC](https://keycitycreativecenter.org/) is Dubuque's collaborative makerspace where creativity comes to life. From woodworking and welding to 3D printing, pottery and textiles, you'll have access to expert guidance, specialized equipment and a community built around learning and making.

![Dupaco logo](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Graphics/Logos/Dupaco%20logos/favicon-152.png?width=150&height=150&name=favicon-152.png)

#### Suported by Dupaco

Dupaco is proud to invest in the people and ideas that help communities thrive. Through this program, we're helping create opportunities to build skills, grow confidence and transform a creative vision into something meaningful.
