## Dupaco's 77th Annual Membership Meeting has ended

##### FAQs

## Have questions? We have answers.

- [**What's a member meeting? What can I expect?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

The annual member meeting is your chance to hear from your credit union leadership team and Board of Directors on how Dupaco is performing. You’ll hear the latest Dupaco news and get up to speed on our recent accomplishments.

- [**What time does the event start?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

The meeting starts at 5:30 p.m. CT on Tuesday, February 24, 2026.

- [**Is there a fee to attend?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

There is no fee to attend.

- [**How do I register for the virtual event?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Register[here](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting)to attend the virtual event.

- [**How do I join the virtual event?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Your unique link to join will be sent to the email used to register for the event. You'll also be able to register on the day of the event by visiting

[www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting.](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting)

Here are a few tips to help you prepare:

Make sure your unique link is ready to join the meeting. If you registered for the virtual event, you’ll receive your unique link by email after February 19, 2026 from GoToWebinar. Hint: GoToWebinar is the platform we're using to host the virtual event.

Make sure your audio and video are working. [Click here](https://support.goto.com/webinar/help#__browse-tree-title-install-and-sign-in--system-requirements) to test your device

- [**I lost my registration email. What should I do?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Sit tight! We'll send a reminder to the email address used to register for the event an hour before the virtual meeting begins. This email will include your unique link to join the meeting

- [**Where can I read the Impact Report?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

You can download your digital version of the 2025 Impact Report by [clicking here](https://www.dupaco.com/about#aboutimpactreport)!

- [**How do I sign up to win prizes?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Members 18 years of age and older are eligible to enter to win prize(s) when [registering](https://register.gotowebinar.com/register/8144699904764717657) for the virtual event.

- [**How can I vote?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Sorry, voting ended on February 13, 2026.

During the voting period, members could vote online through the Credit Union Executive Society (CUES) or by paper ballot (upon request).

- [**Will the meeting be recorded?**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

Yes! A recording of the 77th Annual Membership Meeting will be available to watch on [www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting) after February 24, 2026.

- [**Help! I need technical support**](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/dupacos-77th-annual-membership-meeting#)

For technical support, please [contact GoToWebinar](https://support.goto.com/webinar/contact).

If you are having issues with sound or video, you can test your device [here](https://support.goto.com/webinar/help/what-is-goto-network-test)
