# Building Better Communities

Community isn’t just something we support—it’s who we are. Community is at the center of everything we do. It keeps us grounded, fuels our people-helping-people philosophy and strengthens our commitment to the places we call home. As a not-for-profit financial cooperative, we put people before profits—and reinvest in our members and communities every chance we get.

##### The Dupaco Foundation

## Building on a Legacy of Generosity

Established in 2011 to honor long-time President and CEO Robert W. "Bob" Hoefer, the Dupaco R.W. Hoefer Foundation carries forward a tradition of helping hardworking people navigate unforeseen life events. Your donations provide the financial coaching, education and opportunities to help those in need. You can mail your donations to: Dupaco Foundation, P.O. Box 179, Dubuque, IA 52004-0179.
