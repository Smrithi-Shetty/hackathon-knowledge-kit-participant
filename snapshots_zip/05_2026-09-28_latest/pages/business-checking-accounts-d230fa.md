## Frequently Asked Questions

- [What types of business checking accounts does Dupaco offer?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

We offer several business checking account options, including Business Free Checking, Business 1st Rate Checking and Business Analysis Checking—so you can choose the account that best fits your business size, balance and transaction needs.

- [Is my business checking account federally insured?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

Yes. Your business checking account at Dupaco is federally insured up to $250,000 per account by the National Credit Union Administration.

- [Are there monthly fees for business checking accounts?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

Some business checking accounts have no monthly fees, while others may have fees that can be reduced or avoided by maintaining certain balances. We’ll help you choose the option that makes the most sense for your business.

- [Can I manage my business checking online?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

Absolutely. All Dupaco business checking accounts include free Shine Online and Mobile Banking, so you can monitor cash flow, pay bills, deposit checks and manage your account anytime, anywhere.

- [Can I connect my business checking account to QuickBooks?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

Yes. Dupaco business checking accounts offer Direct Connect to QuickBooks, making it easier to track transactions and keep your accounting up to date.

- [Who can open a business checking account at Dupaco?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/checking-accounts#)

Any eligible business owner—including start-ups, small businesses and established companies—can open a business checking account. Our business services team can help you find the right fit and walk you through the next steps.
