# Dupaco Credit Union

Dupaco is a member-owned,
not-for-profit financial cooperative.
Vision is to build a brighter community for all by
empowering people and strengthening
communities.
We invest in what strengthens our communities
and the nonprofits that make this work possible.
The Innovation Lab is a coworking space that’s
fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in
Eastern Iowa.
This includes coworking spaces, innovation services,
meeting spaces, and team-building activities.
Everything we do helps non-profits launch, grow,
and remain competitive.

```markdown
# Gift of Innovation Grant Program
```

---

### We invest in what strengthens our communities—affordable housing, upskilling, and

### small business development—while also supporting the nonprofits that make this

### work possible.

### Gift of Innovation is NOT limited to nonprofits doing work in Dupaco’s three focus

### areas. It’s designed to help all nonprofits in our communities grow and thrive—by

### offering opportunities to build skills, spark new ideas, and strengthen their impact.

### Gift of Innovation is NOT a monetary award.

### Must be an active 501c3 Public Charity and reside/operate in Dupaco’s charter area.

# Key Points of Program

---

# Full-day Session

### This process enables teams to engage a

### diverse range of stakeholders,

### collaboratively develop 2–3 highly

### actionable strategies, and dive deep into

### crafting detailed implementation plans.

### This is a perfect way to conduct a

### concise, innovation driven session to

### create new ideas & an action plan to

### drive a program, marketing, or

### operational strategy.

### Engages 12-50 stakeholders

### Delivered in-person or virtually

### Helps create 2-3 “winning” ideas AND

### an implementation plan

---

# Two-day Session

### A full innovation process that results in

### innovation-driven strategic plans.

### Most nonprofits know strategic plans are

### important but are often let down by the

### traditional process and static plans they

### produce.

### This session delivers a fresh, effective way to

### conduct strategic planning that puts

### organizational innovation at the center.

### Engages 6-40 stakeholders

### Delivered in-person or virtually

### Results in highly actionable ideas

### AND strategic plan with clear

### implementation steps

---

# One-year Capacity Builder

### During the one-year process, we’ll

### deliver a tailored combination of

### services to help you look under

### the hood to help you build a more

### sustainable organization.

### We’ll map out a process with the

### nonprofit at the start of the

### program.

### One-on-one coaching and strategy discussions

to track progress, provide support, and address new opportunities

### Goal setting with the board and key stakeholders

establish clear and measurable milestones

### Group innovation sessions

identify barriers, generate new ideas, engage diverse perspectives, and
empower stakeholders to execute plan

### Business development sessions

identify and improve factors impacting business model and financial health

### Engages 6-40 stakeholders

### Delivered in-person or virtually

# Evaluation Criteria

• Grantmaking preference will be given to organizations that operate in the cities where
Dupaco maintains a physical branch office.
• Organizational readiness and capacity to participate in innovation sessions
• Level of impact the session would have on the organization and their service to
individuals and the community
• Organization’s stated needs fit well with the proposed process and expected outcomes of
session.
• Quality of the application (clearly stated, feasible, and appropriately focused detailing
how the session would impact their organization)
