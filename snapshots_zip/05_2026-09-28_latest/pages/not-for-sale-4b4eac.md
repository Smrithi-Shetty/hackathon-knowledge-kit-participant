# Not for Sale

#### Built to Share. Not to Sell.

Hey, you guys. You've probably noticed a few banks changing names lately. Not us. Dupaco Credit Union was built to share, not to sell. Since 1948, we've been owned by the people who bank here. That's not going to change.
