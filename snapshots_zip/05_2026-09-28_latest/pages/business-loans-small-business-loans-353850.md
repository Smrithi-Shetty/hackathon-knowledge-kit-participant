## Frequently Asked Questions About Small Business Loans

- [What can I use a small business loan for?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

You can use a small business loan for many business needs, like equipment purchases, working capital, inventory or unexpected expenses. If you’re not sure whether your situation qualifies, we’re happy to talk it through with you.

- [How much can I borrow with a small business loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

If you’re eligible, you may borrow up to $50,000. Your approved amount will depend on factors like your business finances, credit history and overall loan purpose.

- [How fast can I get a decision?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

Once we receive your completed application and required documents, our lending team will review everything as quickly as possible. Timing can vary, but we’ll keep you informed every step of the way.

- [Do I need to provide collateral or a guarantee?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

Guarantees are required for our small business loans. Depending on your loan request, additional documentation or collateral may also be needed.

- [What are the repayment terms like?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

Our small business loans come with fixed monthly payments, so you’ll always know what to expect. Loan terms and rates vary based on your application and eligibility.

- [Do I need to be a Dupaco member to apply for a small business loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/loans/small-business-loans#)

Yes, you’ll need to be a Dupaco member to move forward with a small business loan. Not a member yet? We can help you get started—it’s easy.
