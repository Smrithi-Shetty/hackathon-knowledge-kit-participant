# Lean Business Plan

Describe what you do (product/service), and
what you’re really good at (competitive
advantage, USP, why people buy from you).
Outline what do they do really well and
specifically how you beat them.
Outline which of the 80/20 brings in the
majority of sales, and which of these bring
contribute to the best margins.
Outline your A, B and C customers (easiest,
most profitable), and why they buy.
Describe what are you doing to encourage
the ideal lead (content, SEO, social, email,
web, automation, advertising etc).
Outline you demonstrate authenticity,
credibility and customer trust.
Outline how you prevent new entrants or
existing competitors stealing your
customers, from protecting intellectual
property, intellectual assets or strategic
alliances.
Outline in 60 seconds why as a new
customer I should buy from you.
