## Share the Love (and Cash!)

Love being a member? Invite your friends and family to join Dupaco and you could earn $50 for every person you refer [2](https://www.dupaco.com/about/member-services#fine-print).

**How to get started:**

Log in to Shine Online or Mobile Banking and visit your Refer-a-Friend dashboard under ‘My Perks.’ You’ll earn your cash when your referral completes their required steps.

To be an eligible referrer, you must:

- Be a personal member with a primary savings account ($25 minimum balance).

- Have an active Dupaco checking account at the time of referral and payout.

- Your accounts must be in good standing (no loans delinquent by 60 or more days OR a negative checking account balance for 30 or more days at time of payout).
