# Lean Business Planner

[Tools](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/tools) [Planners](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/planners)

![Lean Business Planner](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/business-active-checking-email-header-2024.jpeg)

Don't let the thought of a 40-page document keep you from launching your dream. Think of a lean business plan as a shortcut to a complete business plan, giving you a streamlined framework to outline your vision. It can help you quickly get to the core of your business idea and evaluate its feasibility before you invest significant time and money. [Use this template](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Lean-Business-Plan-Dupaco.pdf) to distill your strategy, identify your target market, and map out your next steps with confidence.
