Disclosure

A home equity line of credit gives you flexible, ongoing access to funds. It works kind of like a credit card—borrow what you need, pay it off and borrow again. Wondering how much you might be able to borrow with a[home equity loan](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/home-equity)or a[HELOC](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/home-equity)? Our free home equity calculator may help you find out.

Here’s how it works:

- Enter your home’s appraised value.
- Enter your remaining mortgage balance.
- Enter your loan to value ratio (the percentage of your home’s appraised value that’s borrowed).

Our home equity calculator will show you how much you may be eligible to borrow.
