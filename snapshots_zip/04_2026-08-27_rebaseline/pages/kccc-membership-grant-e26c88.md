##### **Key City Creative Center Membership Grant**

# Dreamers, Makers and Entrepreneurs: This One’s for You!

Dupaco and Key City Creative Center (KCCC) are offering three six-month membership grants to the Dubuque makerspace. Whether you’re into woodworking, welding, pottery or more, KCCC is your playground for creativity and collaboration.

##### **About the Grant**

## Create. Collaborate. Launch.

Are you a maker, artist or aspiring entrepreneur in the Tri-state area? Dupaco Credit Union and [Key City Creative Center (KCCC)](https://keycitycreativecenter.org/) are teaming up to help you turn your ideas into reality—with three six-month membership grants to KCCC. Whether you’re looking to sharpen your skills or kickstart a small business, this is your chance to access a vibrant makerspace and a supportive community.

![Notepad](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/Notepad.svg)

#### Membership Grant Details

- Three grants available

- Each grant covers six months of KCCC membership

- Open to anyone with a passion for making, learning or launching a business

![Nut](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/Nut.svg)

#### About Key City Creative Center

KCCC, located in Dubuque, Iowa, is a creative playground for dreamers and doers. Makerspace members get 24/7 access to:

- Woodworking, welding and metalworking tools

- 3D printing, pottery and sewing equipment

- Hands-on classes and workshops

![star](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/star.svg)

#### Why Dupaco is Involved

At Dupaco, we believe in a brighter community for all. That means supporting local creators, entrepreneurs and lifelong learners. This partnership is all about:

- Empowering people to create and innovate

- Supporting small business development

- Helping people upskill and connect with others
