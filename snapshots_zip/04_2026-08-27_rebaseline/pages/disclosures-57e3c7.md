## Patriot Act

To help the government fight the funding of terrorism and money laundering activities, federal law requires all financial institutions to obtain, verify, and record information that identifies each person who opens an account. What this means for you: When you open an account, we’ll ask for your name, address, telephone number, date of birth, Social Security number, employer, drivers license number or picture ID. We may also ask to see a copy of these documents.

[Contact us with questions](https://www.dupaco.com/contact)
