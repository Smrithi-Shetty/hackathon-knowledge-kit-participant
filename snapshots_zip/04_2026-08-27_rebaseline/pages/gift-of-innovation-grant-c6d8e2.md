##### **Gift of Innovation**

# Looking for Solutions for Your Nonprofit?

Dupaco’s Gift of Innovation is a grant program that offers innovation sessions for eligible nonprofit organizations to grow operations and capacity.

![InnovationLab-Dupaco-Lockup-600x160](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/InnovationLab-Dupaco-Lockup-600x160.png?width=600&height=160&name=InnovationLab-Dupaco-Lockup-600x160.png)

#### Application period: Oct. 1–Nov. 12, 2026

## Innovation's Key for Nonprofits to Flourish

Dupaco’s Gift of Innovation, powered by The Innovation Lab, is offering a variety of innovation sessions for nonprofit organizations (up to $40,000 in value). It’s all about boosting your operations and capacity, ultimately helping the communities and people you serve.

Instead of a traditional monetary award, this grant provides a range of innovation sessions: from 1-day sessions to a full year of capacity-building services.

The grant is designed to help nonprofits overcome resource constraints that may limit their potential, and each innovation session is tailored to meet the unique needs of the nonprofit. Innovation sessions can help organizations with challenges such as strategic planning, process development, financial sustainability and business model improvements.

##### **Details & Eligibility**

## Want to Apply? Here's What to Know

- [Eligibility](https://www.dupaco.com/gift-of-innovation-grant#)

  - Applicant must have an active 501(c)(3) status.

  - Applicant must be 501(c)(3) public charity that is not a supporting organization or unit of state, local or federal government (e.g., public library, public university).

  - Applicant agrees to abide by the program award timeline.

  - Applicant submits a completed application in accordance with the procedures.

  - Applicant resides and operates in Dupaco Credit Union’s charter area:
    - **Iowa:** Any county

    - **Illinois:** Carroll, Jo Daviess, Rock Island, Stephenson, Whiteside

    - **Wisconsin:** Columbia, Crawford, Dane, Dodge, Grant, Green, Iowa, Jefferson, Lafayette, Richland, Rock, Sauk, Walworth, Waukesha

_Note: Your organization does not need to be aligned with Dupaco’s community pillars/focus areas to apply for this grant._

- [Application process](https://www.dupaco.com/gift-of-innovation-grant#)

To be considered for a grant, an applicant must:

  - Meet the stated eligibility criteria.

  - Submit a completed grant application at the link below.

  - Provide any supplemental information needed to substantiate that the applicant meets the application requirements.

  - Agree that the organization has the capacity to commit the resources required for the Innovation Session(s) and timeline of the award.

  - Receive a grant if the application is approved pursuant to the Credit Union’s grantmaking selection process.

- [Evaluation criteria](https://www.dupaco.com/gift-of-innovation-grant#)

Grantmaking preference will be given to organizations that operate in the cities where Dupaco maintains a [physical branch office](https://www.dupaco.com/contact#dupaco-locator-widget_1769206785258). Eligible grant applications will be evaluated on the following criteria:

  - Organizational readiness and capacity to participate in innovation sessions.

  - Level of impact the session would have on the organization and their service to individuals and the community.

  - Level of impact the session would have on individuals served (e.g., improved service, new service, increased number of people served).

  - Organization’s stated needs fit well with the proposed process and expected outcomes of session.

  - Quality of the application (clearly stated, feasible, and appropriately focused, detailing how the session would impact the organization).

- [Selection process](https://www.dupaco.com/gift-of-innovation-grant#)

Dupaco Credit Union’s community impact team will review applications and confirm that applicants meet the eligibility criteria. A committee made up of the credit union and our partner, The Innovation Lab, will review and make final approval of award winners.

Applicants may, at the discretion of the Credit Union, be given an opportunity to provide additional information to supplement their application. The Credit Union strives to promptly notify any applicants who are not eligible to receive a Grant due to a failure to meet the eligibility criteria.

The Credit Union anticipates applications will be reviewed and acted upon within eight weeks of submission, however this timeframe may vary based on the time of year and number of applications being submitted. Applicants will be notified by email whether their application was selected for an award.

All grantmaking decisions are subject to the discretion of Dupaco Credit Union. Grantmaking decisions are final and may not be appealed. Dupaco anticipates awarding grants up to $40,000 in value. The actual number and total value of these awards may vary based on the applications received and the interest in specific sessions.
