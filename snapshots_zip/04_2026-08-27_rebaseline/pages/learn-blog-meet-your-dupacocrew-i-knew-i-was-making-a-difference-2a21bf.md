# Meet your \#DupacoCrew: I knew I was making a difference

[Community](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/community)

![Meet your #DupacoCrew: I knew I was making a difference](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/BreonHawthorne-CrewStory-1-sm-20250428-1.jpg)

_By: Breon Hawthorne \| Compliance representative_

MARION, Iowa—Before I was an employee at Dupaco, I was a member. I was working at a grocery store, and when I would visit a Dupaco branch, I always noticed something different about Dupaco employees. Every employee always seemed to have a genuine sense of purpose and fulfillment—regardless of their role.

When I joined Dupaco’s Member Experience team in early 2020, I “floated” to branches and several departments where I was able to jump in and lend a helping hand. Whether I was helping on the front line or in the mortgage department, I was constantly learning and always felt supported.

I knew the work I was doing was important and I was making a difference in our members’ lives.

Eventually, I transitioned into consumer lending and now have found my way into compliance. Although I’m no longer on the front lines helping members directly, the work I do ensures our credit union runs smoothly and continues to serve them effectively. One of the most rewarding aspects of my job is the relationships I’ve built—with members and co-workers.

I see it firsthand when I’m out in the community and members come up to talk with me. They share their stories and thank me for how I was able to help.

I’m also very thankful for the relationships I’ve built with my co-workers and leadership. I’m constantly learning from experienced professionals who push me to grow.

And that’s the beauty of Dupaco: You’re empowered to evolve, to challenge yourself and to truly make a difference.
