Growing your bottom line doesn't always mean making massive, sweeping changes to your business model; often, it's about strategic, incremental adjustments. The [5 Ways to Increase Profit Margins Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/5-ways-to-increase-profit-margins-dupaco) allows you to play with different scenarios—like slightly adjusting your pricing, reducing direct costs, or increasing sales volume—to see the exact impact on your profitability. It’s an eye-opening tool for finding hidden revenue in your current operations.
