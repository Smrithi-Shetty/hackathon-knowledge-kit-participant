## Student Debt Refinance

Simplify your finances by combining federal and private loans into one easy monthly payment. You can refinance up to **$125,000** in student loan debt. Refinancing may help you lower your interest rate or pay off your debt sooner.

**What you need to know:**

- **Eligibility:** You must be a graduate of an [approved public or private not-for-profit school](https://dupaco.studentchoice.org/approved-undergraduate-schools/) and be in the grace or repayment period.

- **Warning:** If you refinance federal loans, you may lose benefits such as income-driven repayment, loan forgiveness, and unemployment deferment.

[Learn more about refinancing](https://dupaco.studentchoice.org/approved-undergraduate-schools/)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- [Who is eligible for the Extra Credit Student Loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

Applicants must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, eligible for credit union membership, and continually enrolled in a degree-granting program at an [approved undergraduate school](https://dupaco.studentchoice.org/approved-undergraduate-schools/).

- [Is there a fee to apply?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

No. There is no origination fee and no prepayment fee if you choose to pay off your loan early.

- [What can I use the loan funds for?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

Funds can be used for "gap" expenses not covered by federal aid, such as tuition, room and board, textbooks, and lab fees.

- [Can I defer my payments while in school?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

Yes. You can defer principal and interest payments while you are enrolled in school.

- [Can I refinance federal loans?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

Yes, but proceed with caution. Refinancing federal loans into a private loan means you will lose federal protections like income-driven repayment and forgiveness programs.

- [What are the refinance limits?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/student-loans#)

You can refinance up to $125,000 in student loan debt. You must be a graduate of an approved not-for-profit school to qualify.
