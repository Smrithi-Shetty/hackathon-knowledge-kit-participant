Disclosure

Wondering how long your retirement savings will last? You’re not alone! We want to ensure that our savings stretch far enough to support the retirement lifestyle we’ve imagined. Whether you’ve made it to retirement—or will be there soon—our free calculator gives you a feel for how long your money could last. The estimate is based on your savings, inflation and other factors.

Ready to find out how long your retirement savings could last? Simply enter this info into the calculator:

- Your estimated retirement savings
- Your expected annual retirement spending
- Your projected after-tax rate of return on your investments
- Expected inflation rate

You can also include your expected deposits and withdrawals to get a more accurate estimate. Feel free to play with the numbers to see how changes in your savings or spending impact how long your money lasts.
