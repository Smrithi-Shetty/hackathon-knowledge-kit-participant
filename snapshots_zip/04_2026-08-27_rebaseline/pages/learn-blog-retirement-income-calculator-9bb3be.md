Transitioning from earning a paycheck to living off your savings can feel daunting without a clear forecast. Use our [Retirement Income Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/retirement-income) to easily estimate your potential monthly income during retirement based on your current savings and expected contributions. It helps you visualize how your nest egg will translate into everyday spending power once you stop working. See if you are on track to maintain your desired lifestyle, or if you need to adjust your strategy today.
