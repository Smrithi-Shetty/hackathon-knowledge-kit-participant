# Rent vs. Buy Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Homeownership](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/homeownership)

![Rent vs. Buy Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Homeownership-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

The decision to continue renting or purchase a home is one of the biggest financial choices you will ever make. Use our [Rent vs Buy Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/rent-vs-buy) to determine if buying or renting makes more financial sense for your specific situation and timeline. By comparing your current rent against potential mortgage costs, taxes, and home appreciation, you can see the long-term impact on your wealth. Get the data you need to decide if you are ready to make the leap into homeownership.
