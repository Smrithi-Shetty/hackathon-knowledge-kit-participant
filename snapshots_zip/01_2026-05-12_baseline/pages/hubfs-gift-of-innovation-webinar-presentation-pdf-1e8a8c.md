Half-day Innovation Session

A fast, effective service that allows a nonprofit to engage a diverse group of stakeholders (volunteers, board members, staff, clients, etc.) in a memorable experience that results in 2-3 highly actionable strategies.

Engages 12-50 stakeholders

Delivered in-person or virtually

Helps create 2-3 “winning” ideas

* * *

Full-day Session

It incorporates all the amazing benefits of the half-day session, while allowing teams to take a deep dive into developing implementation plans.

This is a perfect way to conduct a concise, innovation driven session to create new ideas & an action plan to drive a program, marketing, or operational strategy.

Engages 12-50 stakeholders

Delivered in-person or virtually

Helps create 2-3 “winning” ideas AND actional implementation plan

* * *

Two-day Strategic Session

A full innovation process that results in innovation-driven strategic plans.

Most nonprofits know strategic plans are important but are often let down by the traditional process and static plans they produce.

This session delivers a fresh, effective way to conduct strategic planning that puts organizational innovation at the center.

Engages 6-40 stakeholders

Delivered in-person or virtually

Results in highly actional ideas AND strategic plan with clear implementation steps

* * *

One-year Capacity Builder

During the one-year process, we’ll deliver a tailored combination of services to help you look under the hood to help you build a more sustainable organization.

We’ll map out a process with the nonprofit at the start of the program.

Engages 6-40 stakeholders

Delivered in-person or virtually

One-on-one coaching and strategy discussions
to track progress, provide support, and address new opportunities

Goal setting with the board and key stakeholders
establish clear and measurable milestones

Group innovation sessions
identify barriers, generate new ideas, engage diverse perspectives, and empower stakeholders to execute plan

Business development sessions
identify and improve factors impacting business model and financial health

Eligibility

Active 501(c)(3) public charity
Not a supporting organization or unit of state, local or federal government (e.g., public library, public university)

Dupaco Charter Area
Iowa: Any county. Illinois: Carroll, Jo Daviess, Rock Island, Stephenson, Whiteside. Wisconsin: Columbia, Crawford, Dane, Dodge, Grant, Green, Iowa, Jefferson, Lafayette, Richland, Rock, Sauk, Walworth, Waukesha

Award Timeline
Half-Day, Full-Day, Two-Day Sessions
Completed within 12-months of award
One Year Capacity Builder
Launched within 3-months of award

* * *

Evaluation Criteria

• Grantmaking preference will be given to organizations that operate in the cities where Dupaco maintains a physical branch office.
• Organizational readiness and capacity to participate in innovation sessions
• Level of impact the session would have on the organization and their service to individuals and the community
• Organization’s stated needs fit well with the proposed process and expected outcomes of session.
• Quality of the application (clearly stated, feasible, and appropriately focused detailing how the session would impact their organization)

The Gift of Innovation is a grant program that offers innovation sessions for nonprofit organizations.

Applications will only be accepted during this timeframe: October 3 - November 15, 2024 (11:59 p.m.).

To be considered for a grant, an applicant must meet the following eligibility requirements and follow the application process:

ELIGIBILITY

Applicant will be eligible to receive a grant if they meet the following eligibility criteria:

• Applicant must have an active 501(c)(3) status.
• Applicant must be 501(c)(3) public charity that is not a supporting organization or unit of state, local or federal government (e.g., public library, public university).
