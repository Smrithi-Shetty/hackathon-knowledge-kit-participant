## Frequently asked questions to get you started:

- [What is a business line of credit?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




A business line of credit gives you access to funds you can use as needed. You can borrow, repay and borrow again—up to your approved limit.

- [How is a business line of credit different from a loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




With a traditional loan, you receive a lump sum all at once. A line of credit lets you draw only what you need, when you need it, and you pay interest only on the amount you use.

- [What can I use a business line of credit for?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




Many businesses use a line of credit to manage cash flow, cover short-term expenses, purchase inventory or handle timing gaps between receivables and payables.

- [Do I pay interest on the full credit limit?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




No. Interest is charged only on the amount you’ve borrowed, not on the full credit line.

- [How do payments work?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




Payments are made monthly and are based on your average balance. You can make payments by phone, in person or through Shine Online and Mobile Banking.

- [How do I access the funds?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




You can move funds in real time to your Dupaco Checking or Savings account through Shine.

- [What do I need to apply?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




You’ll need three years of personal and business tax returns, along with a completed loan application and personal financial statement (PFS).

- [How long does the application process take?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




Once your completed application and documents are submitted, our lending team will review them and follow up with next steps.

- [Who can I contact if I have questions?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/credit-lines#)




We’re here to help. You can call us at **800-373-7600, ext. 234**, or share your info and we’ll reach out.
