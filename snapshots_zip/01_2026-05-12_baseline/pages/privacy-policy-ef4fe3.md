# Your Privacy Rights

All financial companies, including Dupaco Community Credit Union, need to share members’ personal information to do business and serve you. Protecting that information is a priority for us. We will not share nonpublic information about you with third parties outside of the Dupaco corporate family, except as explained in our Privacy Policy.

![ID-Security-HGicon-676x338](https://www.dupaco.com/hs-fs/hubfs/ID-Security-HGicon-676x338.jpeg?width=2000&height=1000&name=ID-Security-HGicon-676x338.jpeg)

## Managing Your Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. So does your right to understand and control how your information is used. Our Privacy Notice explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and how we may share it. Our Privacy Policy goes deeper, detailing how we safeguard that information and the choices you have. If you'd like to limit certain types of sharing, just give us a call at 800-373-7600.

[View Our Privacy Notice](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/DupacoCU_Privacy_Policy_2025.pdf?hsLang=en)

[View our Privacy Policy](https://www.dupaco.com/digital-privacy-policy?hsLang=en)

## California Consumer Act

The [California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) disclosure](https://www.dupaco.com/disclosures/california-consumer-privacy-act/?hsLang=en) explains how Dupaco Community Credit Union collects, uses, and discloses personal information relating to California residents. **If you are a California resident, you may tell Dupaco [Do Not Sell My Personal Information](https://www.dupaco.com/disclosures/california-consumer-privacy-act#do-not-sell-personal).**
