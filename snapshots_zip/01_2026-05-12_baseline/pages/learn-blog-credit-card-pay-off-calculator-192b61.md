# Credit Card Pay Off Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Borrowing & Credit](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/borrowing-credit)

![Credit Card Pay Off Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/credit-cards-header-graphic-2.webp)

Paying only the minimum on your credit cards can keep you trapped in debt for years. Use our [Credit Card Pay Off Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/credit-card-pay-off) to see exactly how much you should pay each month to eliminate your balance on your own timeline. By visualizing the massive amount of interest you save by increasing your monthly payments, you'll be motivated to tackle your debt aggressively. Build a clear, actionable payoff plan and take back control of your finances.
