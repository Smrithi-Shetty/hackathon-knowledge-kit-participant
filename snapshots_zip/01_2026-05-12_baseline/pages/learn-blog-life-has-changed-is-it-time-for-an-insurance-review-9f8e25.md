# Life has changed. Is it time for an insurance review?

[Insurance](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/insurance)

![Life has changed. Is it time for an insurance review?](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/resources-general-email-graphic-scaled-Jan-24-2026-08-13-33-5527-PM.jpg)

If your income or lifestyle has changed, it might be a good time to review your [insurance](https://www.dupaco.com/insure).

Checking your coverage annually or any time you experience a life event helps ensure you’re adequately insured. And it might save you money.

You’ll want to provide your agent with your current declaration, or dec, page. This document offers an important snapshot of your coverage, including your limits, deductibles, endorsements, premiums and discounts.

Not sure whether something warrant an insurance review? Here are five changes that could affect your insurance needs:

## **Decreased income**

If your [budget](https://www.dupaco.com/budgeting) has taken a hit, are your premiums still affordable? It’s important to protect your assets—at a price you can afford.

### ![How can I lower my insurance costs?](https://www.dupaco.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-lower-cost-graphic-676x338-web.jpg?width=676&height=338&name=IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-lower-cost-graphic-676x338-web.jpg)

## **Driving less**

Maybe this is the year you became a work-from-home employee. Or your college-bound student plans to leave the car at home.

Driving significantly less might mean big savings for your premiums. It’s worth asking your insurance agent.

## **Home entertainment**

Many of us have found new ways to have fun at home.

If you’ve purchased a trampoline, pool or other significant investment, check in with your agent. Some homeowners policies come with restrictions, while others simply want to document your new asset.

### ![What can I do to make sure that I have enough insurance?](https://www.dupaco.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-coverage-graphic-676x338-web.jpg?width=676&height=338&name=IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-coverage-graphic-676x338-web.jpg)

## **Home renovation**

Spending more time at home might mean you’re tackling long-awaited projects.

The bigger ones, like finishing your basement, can increase the value of your home. The problem? Your original homeowners policy doesn’t reflect the additional square footage, so it might not cover your full replacement cost.

[Learn how to use the equity in your home now >](https://www.dupaco.com/2019/07/17/home-equity-loan-or-line-of-credit-which-is-right-for-you/)

### ![What is replacement cost?](https://www.dupaco.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-replacement-graphic-676x338-web.jpg?width=676&height=338&name=IAMT-intro-to-insurance-QA-replacement-graphic-676x338-web.jpg)

## **Other events worth an insurance review**

Life continues to happen.

So if you’ve celebrated the birth of a child, marriage, new teen or permit driver or another monumental family milestone, reach out to your insurance agent.

You’ll want to make sure your insurance still reflects your needs.
