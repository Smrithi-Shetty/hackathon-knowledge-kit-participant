# Business Loan Repayment Calculator

[Tools & Templates](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/tools-templates) [Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/calculators)

![Business Loan Repayment Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/calculator-hero-graphic-icon-web-template.jpg)

Every dollar you save on interest is a dollar you can reinvest back into growing your business. If you currently have outstanding debt, the [Business Loan Repayment Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/business-loan-repayment-calculator-dupaco) is an incredibly empowering tool. It allows you to experiment with your payment schedule by plugging in extra monthly or yearly principal payments. You'll see exactly how much money you can save in interest and how much faster you can become debt-free.
