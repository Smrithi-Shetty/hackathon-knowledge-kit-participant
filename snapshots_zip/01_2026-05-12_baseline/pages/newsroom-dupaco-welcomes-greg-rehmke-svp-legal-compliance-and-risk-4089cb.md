# Dupaco welcomes Greg Rehmke, SVP Legal Compliance and Risk

[Press Releases](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/press-releases) [Dupaco Updates](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/dupaco-updates)

![Dupaco welcomes Greg Rehmke, SVP Legal Compliance and Risk](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/placeholder-no-image.png)

Dubuque, Iowa – Dupaco Credit Union is pleased to welcome Greg Rehmke to the financial cooperative as SVP, Legal, Compliance and Risk. In this new role, Rehmke will further strengthen the organization’s commitment to excellence in governance and risk management.

Rehmke brings more than 17 years of experience to Dupaco, having previously held key positions in both internal and external legal roles representing financial institutions. His extensive background and leadership skills will be instrumental in guiding Dupaco through the evolving landscape of financial services.

In addition to his professional experience, Greg currently serves as President of the Dubuque Symphony Orchestra.

“We are thrilled to welcome Greg to the Dupaco team,” said Joe Hearn, Dupaco President/CEO. “His expertise and vision will be invaluable as we continue to grow and innovate. Greg’s role is pivotal in ensuring we uphold the highest standards of legal and risk management.”

###

ABOUT DUPACO COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION

Dupaco Community Credit Union is a not-for-profit, member-owned financial cooperative headquartered in Dubuque, Iowa. It is dedicated to the financial well-being of its members, and specializes in personalized financial counseling, money advice and education. It offers savings, loans, investments, insurance and wealth management products for individuals and businesses. Dupaco serves residents in 118 counties throughout Iowa, northwest Illinois and southwest Wisconsin. Founded in 1948 by ten Dubuque Packing Company employees, membership has grown to more than 173,000 with assets exceeding $3.5 billion. It has over 600 employees and 23 branch office locations. It is a Forbes Best-in-State Credit Union, Forbes America’s Best Small Employer, and a Des Moines Register Top Workplace. Learn more at www.dupaco.com.
