# Roth vs. Traditional IRA Calculator

[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Investing & Retirement](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/investing-retirement)

![Roth vs. Traditional IRA Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Retirement-goals-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Transitioning from earning a paycheck to living off your savings can feel daunting without a clear forecast. Use our [Roth vs. Traditional IRA Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/roth-vs-traditional-ira) to easily estimate your potential monthly income during retirement based on your current savings and expected contributions. It helps you visualize how your nest egg will translate into everyday spending power once you stop working. See if you are on track to maintain your desired lifestyle, or if you need to adjust your strategy today.
