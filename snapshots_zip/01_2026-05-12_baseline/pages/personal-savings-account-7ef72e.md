## Frequently Asked Questions

- [Is a money market account better than a traditional savings account?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




It depends on your stash. If you typically keep a higher balance (at least $2,500), a money market could offer a higher dividend rate to help your money grow faster. If you’re just starting out, a traditional savings account might be your best bet. Curious just how much you could earn? Try our free [Compound Interest Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/compounding-interest-calculator/?hsLang=en) to crunch the numbers!

- [Should I choose a certificate (CD) or a money market?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




Think of it as certainty vs. access. If you have a lump sum you don’t need for a few months or years, a term-share certificate locks in a higher, guaranteed rate. If you want a higher rate but need to move money occasionally, a money market gives you that flexibility without the lock-in period. You might find that a combination of both works best for you!

- [How easy is it to get to my money in a money market account?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




Very! While you can’t write checks from a Dupaco money market account, you can transfer funds instantly in Shine Online or Mobile Banking or [visit us](https://www.dupaco.com/locations?hsLang=en) in person. There’s a minimum withdrawal of $250. But there are no penalties for withdrawals made in-branch. Plus, you can transfer money online between accounts without penalty.

- [Can I lose money in a money market account?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




Your deposits are federally insured for at least $250,000 by the National Credit Union Administration. Curious how your Dupaco accounts are insured? You can find out by calling us at 800-373-7600, ext. 206.

- [Can I add an authorized user to my HSA?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




Yes! You can designate one additional authorized user, like a spouse or caregiver, who will get their own HSA MoneyCard to pay for medical expenses out of your HSA. Just remember, as the account owner, it’s your responsibility to make sure those withdrawals are for IRS-qualified medical expenses.

- [How do I put money into my HSA?](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-account#)




You can set up direct deposit or payroll deductions to save automatically (and often pre-tax!). You can also move money at any [branch](https://www.dupaco.com/locations?hsLang=en) or via mail deposit. Because the IRS requires a signed form for HSA contributions, these deposits can’t be made through Shine. Please contact us regarding annual HSA contribution limits.
