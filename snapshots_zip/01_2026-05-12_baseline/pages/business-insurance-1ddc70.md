### Liability Insurance

Ensure one accident doesn’t turn into a major setback.

- Protect your business if someone's injured or their property's damaged.
- Help cover legal costs and claims in case of an accident.
- The coverage is often required by partners or contracts.

![buildingOffice](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/buildingOffice.svg)

### Property Insurance

A financial safety net so your business can recover faster.

- Help cover damage to your building, equipment and inventory.
- Protect against losses from fire, storms, theft and other covered events.
- Have peace of mind that your space and tools are protected.

![UsersFour](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/UsersFour.svg)

### Worker’s Compensation

Protect your employees and your business if an injury happens.

- Help cover medical costs and lost wages if an employee's injured on the job.
- Safeguard your employees and your business at the same time.
- It's often required by law once you have employees.

![treestructure](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/icons/treestructure.svg)
