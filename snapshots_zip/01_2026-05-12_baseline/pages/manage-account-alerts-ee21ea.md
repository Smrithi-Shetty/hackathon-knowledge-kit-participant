##### I am a Dupaco member

## Need to change how you receive two-step verification codes?

Two-step verification is a requirement for all Shine Online Banking accounts. We're unable to remove this security feature from your account as well as the alerts that follow.

If you don’t want to receive your two-step codes via email, add your mobile number to receive codes via text instead.

##### **How to receive codes via text**

If you don’t want to receive your 2-Step codes via email, add your mobile number to receive codes via text instead. You can do so by updating your settings in either Shine Mobile or Shine Online.

**In Shine Mobile:**

Once you’re logged in, you can add your number via the “Two Step Notification” option of your Profile. Select “Add contact”, enter your number, and follow the prompts.

**In Shine Online:**

Once you’re logged in, mouse over “Profile”, select on “Edit Profile”. Under the “Actions” menu on the right hand side, select “edit two-step” and follow the prompts on screen.

_Hint: These settings can be changed again later at any time._

##### **How to skip code entry**

If you don’t want to enter codes at all, follow these steps.

**In Shine Mobile:**

Enable face or fingerprint recognition or setup PIN Login. Once you’re logged in, you can configure either option from the Device Security section of your Profile.

Hint: Face and fingerprint recognition can’t be activated unless your device supports those options.

**In Shine Online:**

Choose “Trust” this device” (on your login screen). Doing so secures your account with the most recent login code entered.

_Hint: Not recommended for computers or devices you don’t own._

### Want to turn off or adjust transaction, balance or statement alerts?

To manage these alerts please login to [Shine Online Banking](https://shine.dupaco.com/) and navigate to the eNotifiers section where you can configure which alerts you would like to receive.

## Received an Alert by Mistake

- [Received a verification code you didn't request?](https://www.dupaco.com/manage-account-alerts#)




If you received one of these verification codes it’s likely that one of our members mistyped their phone number or email address when registering. This shouldn’t happen again but if you continue to receive these codes please give us a call at 800-373-7600, ext. 0 or contact us at [service@dupaco.com](mailto:service@dupaco.com).

- [Received a transaction or account alert that isn't yours?](https://www.dupaco.com/manage-account-alerts#)




Please give us a call at 800-373-7600, ext. 0 or contact us at [service@dupaco.com](mailto:service@dupaco.com) and we will remove your number or email from the associated account. Sometimes people change emails or phone numbers and you may have received a new number that once belonged to one of our members.


_Dupaco takes your security and privacy very seriously. So we’ll never ask you to provide personal or account information in an email. And we ask that you please be cautious when opening or responding to emails, text messages, and phone calls related to your Dupaco account(s). If you’re concerned about the legitimacy of a message you receive, please call us at 800-373-7600, ext. 0._
