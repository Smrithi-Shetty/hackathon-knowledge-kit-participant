# Dupaco announces board election results (2021)

[Press Releases](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/press-releases) [Dupaco Updates](https://www.dupaco.com/newsroom/tag/dupaco-updates)

![Dupaco announces board election results (2021)](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/placeholder-no-image.png)

DUBUQUE, Iowa—During Dupaco Community Credit Union’s 72nd Annual Membership meeting held Sunday, Feb. 21, 2021, Jeff Gonner, Ron Mussehl and Randy Skemp were re-elected to serve three-year terms on the credit union’s volunteer board of directors.

Gonner has served on the Dupaco board since 2009. He is the chief financial officer at Medical Associates Clinic in Dubuque. Mussehl joined the Dupaco board in 2010. He is now retired and previous owner of Ron’s BP Convenience Stories in Dubuque. Skemp joined the Dupaco board in 2003. He is vice president of sales at Active Network, LLC based in Dallas, Texas.

Following the membership meeting, the board of directors held its re-organizational meeting, at which these officer appointments were made:

- Andy Schroeder, chair of board
- Ron Meyers, vice chair
- Steve Chapman, secretary
- Renee Poppe, treasurer
- Joe Hearn, president/CEO

These directors were appointed to committee chair positions:

- Steve Chapman, chair, Salary Savings Plan Oversight committee
- Denise Dolan, chair, Personnel committee
- Jeff Gonner, chair, Investment/Asset Liability Management committee
- Ron Mussehl, chair, Business Lending committee
- Renee Poppe, chair, Marketing committee
- Ron Meyers, chair, CUSO Board of Managers
- Randy Skemp, chair, Audit committee
- Bob Wethal, Nomination and Credit/Delinquent Loan committee

As a member-owned, democratically controlled cooperative, all Dupaco members have an equal say in their credit union. Each member has one vote, no matter how much money she or he has in deposits or loans at Dupaco.

**ABOUT DUPACO COMMUNITY CREDIT UNION:**

Dupaco Community Credit Union is a not-for-profit, member-owned financial cooperative. It is dedicated to the financial sustainability of its members, and specializes in customized financial counseling, money advice and education. It offers savings, loans, investments, insurance and wealth management products for individuals and businesses. Dupaco serves residents in 112 counties throughout Iowa, northwest Illinois and southwest Wisconsin. Chartered in 1948, membership totals more than 136,000 with assets exceeding $2.5 billion. Visit Dupaco at [www.dupaco.com](https://www.dupaco.com/).
