## Share the Love (and Cash!)

Love being a member? Invite your friends and family to join Dupaco and you could earn $50 for every person you refer1. To sweeten the deal, we’ll give your referrals the chance to earn $502, too!

**How to get started:**

Log in to Shine Online or Mobile Banking and visit your Refer-a-Friend dashboard under ‘My Perks.’ You’ll earn your cash when your referral completes these three steps:

- Click: They use your unique referral link in their invitation email3.

- Join: They open a new checking account with our online application3.

- Activate: They successfully meet our active checking definition4.


To be an eligible referrer, you must: Be a personal member with a primary savings account ($25 minimum balance) and have an active Dupaco checking account4 at the time of referral and payout. Your accounts must also be in good standing (no loans delinquent by 60 or more days OR a negative checking account balance for 30 or more days at time of payout).

[Start referring in Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/mobile/referFriend?hsLang=en)
