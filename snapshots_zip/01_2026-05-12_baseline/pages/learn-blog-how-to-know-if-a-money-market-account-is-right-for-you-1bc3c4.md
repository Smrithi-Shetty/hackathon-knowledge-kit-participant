# How to know if a money market account is right for you

[Saving](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/saving)

![How to know if a money market account is right for you](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/wordpress-migration/savings-general-email-graphic-scaled-4.jpg)

By Meggan Heacock \| _Senior vice president, finance_

If you’ve been paying yourself first and growing your savings, congratulations!

Eventually, you might reach a point where a traditional [savings account](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-accounts/) isn’t enough, because other avenues can help you save even more.

One saving strategy—a [money market account](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-accounts/money-market/). Knowing how Dupaco’s money market accounts work can help you decide whether it’s a good fit for you.

## **Earn even more with a money market account**

The more you save, the more interest you earn compared to a traditional savings account. You typically start reaping the benefits of a money market account once you’ve saved at least $2,500.

Don’t have that much saved yet? You can get there by creating a Savings Goal within [Shine Online or Mobile Banking](https://www.dupaco.com/connect/online-and-mobile-banking/).

## **Keep saving**

Just like a regular savings account, you can make deposits any time [in person](https://www.dupaco.com/locations), through payroll deduction or by account transfer.

### ![Easy access](https://www.dupaco.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/36_F_Social_Wide_03_US.jpg?width=1280&height=668&name=36_F_Social_Wide_03_US.jpg)

## **Easy access to your money**

Unlike a term-share [certificate](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/savings-accounts/certificates/), you don’t have to commit to saving for a set period. If you have a certificate that’s about to end and don’t want to keep your funds locked up, this can be a great option.

You can withdraw money (a minimum of $250) any time.

## **Not penalized**

You aren’t hit with fees if your balance decreases.

## **Money market accounts are for everyone**

Our money market accounts are a member benefit—for both new and existing members. You don’t have to bring new money to the credit union to take advantage of our competitive, consistent rates.

## **Your money is protected**

Your funds are federally insured by the [National Credit Union Administration](https://www.ncua.gov/).
