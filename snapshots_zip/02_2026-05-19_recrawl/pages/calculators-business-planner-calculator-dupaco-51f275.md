IP can include trademarks, copyright, patents, industrial designs and plant variety rights. For more information go to the [US Patent and Trademark Office](https://www.uspto.gov/).


Identify your core intellectual assets (processes, trade secrets, business know-how, etc.). Outline how you protect them.

- [Learn](https://content.tsbc.com/dupaco/business-plan.html#tabs-61)

An intellectual asset is a competitive advantage that usually can’t be legally protected, such as your business contacts, networks, your business know-how and knowledge of what customers want. It’s different to intellectual property which you can register to own. Intellectual assets tend to be trade secrets or business ‘know how’ such as verbal agreements, internal processes, knowledge of a customer and their buying preferences or budgets, and your own customer behavior data. Protect them by keeping your information secret and locked away, have staff sign confidentiality agreements, document internal processes with key employees only, share sensitive information only to qualified leads and limit what you place online and in the public arena.
