Moving your operations from a leased space into a building you own is a huge milestone that helps you build long-term equity. But commercial real estate mortgages have their own unique variables. The [Business Real Estate Loan Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/business-real-estate-loan-dupaco) is tailored for business owners looking to purchase a storefront, warehouse, or office space. It helps you forecast your commercial mortgage payments and decide if buying is the right financial move for your company.
