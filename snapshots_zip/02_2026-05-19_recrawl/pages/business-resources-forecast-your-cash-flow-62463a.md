# Forecast Your Cashflow

[Templates](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/templates) [Exit](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/exit) [Cash Flow](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/cash-flow) [Grow](https://www.dupaco.com/business/resources/tag/grow)

![Forecast Your Cashflow](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Resources-General-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Running a successful business means navigating unexpected expenses, making financial visibility absolutely critical. It’s important to monitor the cash flow of your business so you can plan ahead and avoid stressful shortfalls. Use this template to forecast your cash flow, track your incoming and outgoing funds, and ensure you always have enough working capital on hand. By mapping out your finances, you can make smarter purchasing decisions and keep your operations running smoothly.
