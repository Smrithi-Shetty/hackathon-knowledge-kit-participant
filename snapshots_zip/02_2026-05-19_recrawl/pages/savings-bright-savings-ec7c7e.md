##### FAQs

## Have questions? We have answers.

Our team of experts has answers to some of your most commonly asked questions. But if you’d rather talk to a human, you can give us a call at [800-373-7600](tel:8003737600). We can’t wait to talk!

- [What is an online-only savings account?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




It’s a savings account you open and manage entirely online—with no branch visits needed. Everything from deposits to transfers can be handled through [Shine Online or Mobile Banking](https://www.dupaco.com/connect/online-and-mobile-banking/?hsLang=en), whenever and wherever it works for you! It’s perfect if you do most of your banking on your phone or computer.



By keeping this account digital-only, it keeps things simple on our end. And we pass those savings on to you with better rates and fewer fees. With Bright Savings, there’s no minimum balance to open, no maintenance fees and no complicated requirements. It’s savings, made simple—just the way it should be.

- [How does an online-only savings account work?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




Your money earns dividends (that’s our version of interest) every day. At the end of each month, your earnings are deposited into your Bright Savings account. And thanks to compounding, you earn dividends on those dividends—helping your savings grow even more over time.



Want to see how it works in action? Try our free [compound interest calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/savings-interest?hsLang=en) to estimate your savings over time.



You can only earn the special Bright Savings rate by enrolling in eStatements. Plus, this account comes with tools to help you save even smarter:



  - Create virtual savings buckets for goals like vacations or car repairs—all within one account.
  - Earn dividends on your total account balance, no matter how it’s split across buckets.
  - Get personalized savings insights to spot new ways to save.
  - Activate Smart Save to let the tool look for safe-to-save amounts and move money into your account automatically.

- [What are saving buckets and how should I use them?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




Savings buckets help you organize your goals inside your Bright Savings account. You can personalize each virtual bucket with a nickname (like “Italy Trip”), a target amount and date, and watch your progress grow. You’ll want to:



  - **Keep it simple:** 1-6 buckets is usually plenty.
  - **Leave a cushion:** Keep some money in Core Savings as a “stash” for life’s surprises.
  - **Split intentionally:** Set split ratios for deposits based on your priorities (they just need to total 100%).
  - **Check in:** Review and adjust your buckets regularly as life happens.
  - **Celebrate:** Once you’ve reached your goal, you can move the money to Core Savings and transfer it out.

- [How do I open a Bright Saving account?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




It’s quick and easy to do online—just [click here](https://join.dupaco.com/products/brightSavings) to get started. The whole process takes just a few minutes. You don’t need to make a minimum deposit to open your account, but you will need to qualify for a [Dupaco membership](https://www.dupaco.com/about/join?hsLang=en) if you’re not already a member.

- [How can this account offer such great rates?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




This online-only account is built for digital life—no branch visits, no paper statements. That keeps things simple on our end, so we can offer you a better rate. Just make sure you’re enrolled in eStatements to earn the special Bright Savings rate!

- [How can I add money to my online-only savings account?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




You have lots of options! You can:



  - Transfer funds from your other Dupaco accounts using[Shine Online or Mobile Banking](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Schedule automatic transfers using[Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Link an external bank account through[Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en)and transfer funds from that account.
  - Set up direct deposit so part of every paycheck goes straight into your account. (You’ll need to work with your employer to get started.)
  - Deposit checks into your account with mobile deposit in[Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Turn on Smart Save to let the tool look for safe-to-save moments and move money into your account automatically.

Just keep in mind there’s a maximum account balance of $1 million.

- [How can I withdraw money from an online savings account?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




You also have lots of options to access your money from this online-only savings account. You can:



  - Transfer funds to one of your other Dupaco accounts using[Shine Online or Mobile Banking](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Transfer funds to someone else’s Dupaco account using[Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Schedule automatic transfers from your Bright Savings account using[Shine](https://www.dupaco.com/shine?hsLang=en).
  - Pay Dupaco loans from your Bright Savings account.
  - Transfer funds to your Dupaco checking account and use your MoneyCard debit card to take out cash from a nearby[ATM](https://www.dupaco.com/contact#dupaco-locator-widget_1769206785258).

Don’t have a Dupaco checking account yet? Learn how to open one [here](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/checking-accounts?hsLang=en).

- [Is an online-only savings account safe?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)


All deposits are federally insured up to $250,000 by the [National Credit Union Administration](https://ncua.gov/).

- [Do I need a checking account to open an online-only savings account?](https://www.dupaco.com/savings/bright-savings#)




Nope! But having both can make things easier to manage your money. Plus, with a checking account, you’ll have easy access to your cash through a debit card, ATMs or check writing.



If you’re looking to earn a little extra,[1st Rate Checking](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/checking-accounts?hsLang=en)pays dividends when you keep a higher balance. Want to keep it simple? Check out our no-fuss[Free Checking](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/checking-accounts?hsLang=en).
