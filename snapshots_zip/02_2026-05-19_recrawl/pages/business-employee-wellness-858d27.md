## Frequently asked questions to get you started:

- [What is Dupaco’s Financial Wellness program?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




It’s a free employee benefit that provides financial education, coaching and resources to help employees feel more confident managing their money—at every stage of life.

- [Does this program cost employers or employees anything?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




No. The program is offered at no cost to employers or employees.

- [Do employees need to be Dupaco members to participate?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




No. The program is open to all employees, regardless of where they bank.

- [How is the program delivered?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




We offer flexible options, including on-site sessions, virtual workshops and on-demand resources, so employees can access support whenever and wherever it works best for them.

- [What topics are covered?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




Topics include budgeting, debt management, saving, retirement planning and other everyday financial decisions employees face.

- [How does financial wellness benefit my business?](https://www.dupaco.com/business/employee-wellness#)




Supporting employees’ financial well-being can help reduce stress, improve engagement, increase productivity and support retention.
