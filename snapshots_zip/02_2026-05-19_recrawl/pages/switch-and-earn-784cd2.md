## Frequently Asked Questions

- [Can I refinance more than one loan?](https://www.dupaco.com/switch-and-earn#)




Yes—you can bring over multiple loans from elsewhere and even combine them into one at Dupaco.

- [Do all of my loans need to be an eligible type?](https://www.dupaco.com/switch-and-earn#)




Nope. As long as one loan is an eligible type (auto, motorcycle, personal or recreational), others can come along if they're eligible for consolidation.

- [How is my cash back calculated?](https://www.dupaco.com/switch-and-earn#)




It’s based on 1% of the loan balance you move to us, up to $300\*. It's our way of saying thanks for bringing your loan to your credit union!

- [When will I get the cash back?](https://www.dupaco.com/switch-and-earn#)




We don't like waiting, and we bet you don't either. You'll see that cash back shortly after your new loan is funded (within 3-5 business days).
