Wondering if refinancing your home is worth it? Our mortgage refinance calculator can help you estimate your new monthly payments and see how much you might save. Many homeowners think about refinancing when interest rates drop or their needs change.

To use our free refinance calculator, simply enter this info:

- Your remaining loan balance
- Your current interest rate
- Your monthly payment
- The new[interest rate](https://www.dupaco.com/personal/loans/home-loans?hsLang=en)you’re considering
