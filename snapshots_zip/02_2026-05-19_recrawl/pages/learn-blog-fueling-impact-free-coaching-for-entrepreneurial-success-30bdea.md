# Fueling impact: Free coaching for entrepreneurial success

[Budgeting](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/budgeting) [Investing & Retirement](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/investing-retirement)

![Fueling impact: Free coaching for entrepreneurial success](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Seminars-educate-HGicon-676x338.jpeg)

Every thriving business starts with a spark—an idea, a dream, a vision. But turning that spark into something sustainable takes more than passion. It takes support, strategy and the right tools. That’s where Dupaco’s partnership with the Innovation Lab and Rural Ideas Network is making a difference.

Through free virtual business coaching and education, Dupaco members are gaining more than just access to services—they’re gaining momentum.

> “We’re not just offering coaching—we’re helping people change their lives,” Michelle Becwar, community and social impact manager at Dupaco, said. “This is about empowering our members to invest in themselves and build their own economic opportunities.”

Whether it’s launching a new venture, navigating growth, or planning for succession, entrepreneurs are receiving the guidance they need to move forward with confidence. Tailored guidance might include one-on-one support from business experts at the Innovation Lab or peer discussions where ideas are bounced off each other. And the results speak for themselves: stronger businesses, smarter decisions, and a growing network of rural innovators who are lifting each other up.

Take for example, Stephanee Johnson, founder of [Organizing Innovations](https://www.organizinginnovations.com/), a home organizing service. Stephanee got support forming her LLC, launching her website, and developing her business model and pricing.

Stephanee felt the team listened to her dream of an idea. “Yes, let’s make it happen,” they said, assisting her with every decision and direction.

> “I feel so fortunate to have found such an amazing service,” Stephanee said, “I could go on and on about how life changing this has been for me, and I would recommend this to anyone looking to start a business.”

## **Real tools, real results**

Here’s how Dupaco members are turning ideas into impact:

- **Startup Coaching:** Members are transforming ideas into action with one-on-one coaching that helps them take the first—and most important—steps.
- **Business Management Coaching:** Entrepreneurs are building more resilient operations with expert guidance on planning, strategy, and leadership.
- **Website Design Bootcamp & Evaluations:** From launching their first site to optimizing existing ones, members are creating digital storefronts that drive growth.
- **Curated Learning Libraries:** With access to top-tier workshops and podcasts, members are sharpening their skills and staying inspired.
- **Succession Planning:** Business owners are preparing for the future—ensuring their legacy continues with thoughtful, strategic transitions.
- **Peer Learning Communities:** Through roundtables and forums, members are connecting with others who understand their challenges and share their drive.

> “This isn’t only about access—it’s about impact,” Jordan DeGree, founder and executive director of the Innovation Lab and Rural Ideas Network, said. “We’re seeing entrepreneurs gain clarity, confidence, and community. That’s what changes everything.”
