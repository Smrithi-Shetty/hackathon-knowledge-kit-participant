[Calculators](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/calculators) [Borrowing & Credit](https://www.dupaco.com/learn/blog/tag/borrowing-credit)

![Debt Consolidation Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/hubfs/Graphics/Collage%20hero%20graphics/Money-HGicon-676x338.jpg)

Juggling multiple high-interest balances can drain your bank account and cause unnecessary stress. Use our [Debt Consolidation Calculator](https://www.dupaco.com/calculators/debt-consolidation) to see exactly what you could save by combining your various debts into one, manageable monthly payment. Consolidating often secures a lower overall interest rate, helping you pay down your principal faster while simplifying your life. Discover your potential savings and map out a faster route to becoming debt-free.
